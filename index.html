What I'm doing now

Management Consulting at Carpedia

I am a management consultant at Carpedia International, where we do a lot of hands-on, on-site work with our clients. We spend much of our time finding outdated, manual processes and working with the people who run them to put something better in place.

I have found that identifying a manual process is usually much easier than getting people to change how they work. People have often done things the same way for a long time, so I spend a lot of my time applying change management principles to help them adopt a new way of thinking.

Finding the problemGetting people to change (the tougher part)
  1. Step 1Map the current process

    I work on site with the people doing the job to understand how it runs today.

  2. Step 2Identify the opportunity

    I look for outdated or manual steps that are taking up people's time.

  3. Step 3Engage the team

    I use change management and stakeholder engagement to get people on board.

  4. Step 4Support the change

    I work with the team as they adopt the new process so that it lasts.
